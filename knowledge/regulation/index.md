# Instruments

* [EU Cyber Resilience Act](cra.md) - Regulation (EU) 2024/2847: an SBOM becomes a legal obligation, floored at top-level dependencies and not required to be published.

# What belongs here

Only instruments that change **what a bill of materials must contain, or when one must exist**. A
cyber-security obligation that never reaches the BOM is out of scope, however significant it is
otherwise.

These concepts describe what an instrument requires *of a document*. They do not say whether it
applies to a given product or organisation — that is a legal question, and not one a reference
corpus should answer.
